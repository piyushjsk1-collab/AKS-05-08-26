Frequently Asked Questions

Some common questions about our interior design services.

How does the interior design process start?

You can contact us through WhatsApp to discuss your project. We can then understand your requirements, space and preferred design direction before discussing the next steps.

Can the design be customized to my budget?

Yes. Material choices, furniture specifications and the overall project scope can be discussed according to your requirements and available budget.

Do you provide 3D designs?

3D visualization can be included depending on the selected service and agreed project scope.

How long does an interior project take?

Project duration varies depending on the size, design scope, material availability and execution requirements. A timeline can be discussed after understanding the project.

Do you provide warranty?

Warranty terms, if applicable, depend on the specific products, materials, hardware and services included in the agreed project. Applicable warranty details should be confirmed before purchase.

Terms & Conditions

Please review the following general terms before engaging our services.

1. Services

Vera Living provides interior design, space planning, visualization, furniture, modular kitchen and related design or execution services depending on the project scope agreed with the client.

2. Project Scope

The exact scope of work, materials, specifications, quantities, deliverables and services will be discussed and confirmed before project execution. Any work outside the agreed scope may be quoted separately.

3. Pricing & Estimates

Initial discussions or estimates are indicative unless specifically confirmed as a final quotation. Final pricing may depend on selected materials, dimensions, design changes, quantities, site conditions, transportation and other project requirements.

4. Design Approval

Design concepts, drawings, layouts, material selections and other project details should be reviewed and approved by the client before execution. Changes requested after approval may affect cost and timeline.

5. Project Timelines

Project timelines depend on the agreed scope, approvals, material availability, site readiness, third-party vendors and other circumstances. Any estimated completion date will be communicated based on the project conditions known at the time.

6. Payments

Payment schedules and amounts will be communicated as part of the project quotation or agreement. Work may be scheduled according to the agreed payment milestones.

7. Materials & Product Availability

Material and product availability may vary. If a selected product is unavailable, alternatives may be discussed with the client before proceeding with a replacement.

8. Warranty

Any warranty offered for a particular product, material, hardware item or service will be subject to the applicable manufacturer or project warranty terms. Warranty coverage should be confirmed in writing before purchase or installation where applicable.

9. Client Responsibilities

The client is responsible for providing accurate project information, access to the site when required, timely approvals and other information reasonably necessary for the project.

10. Portfolio & Photography

Project photographs or completed work may only be used for portfolio, marketing or promotional purposes where appropriate permissions have been obtained from the relevant client.

11. Communication

Project communication may take place through WhatsApp, phone, email or other mutually agreed channels. Important commercial decisions, approvals and changes should be documented appropriately.

12. General

These general terms are provided for informational purposes. Specific project agreements, quotations and written confirmations may contain additional terms applicable to an individual project.
